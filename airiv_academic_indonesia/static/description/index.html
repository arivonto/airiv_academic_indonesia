Overview

Comprehensive Indonesian Academic, School, and Campus Management ERP Suite for Odoo 18 Community Edition. Equipped with an integrated Universal Government Integration Bridge for Indonesian regulatory reporting: 1. PDDikti Neo Feeder REST Gateway (Universitas, Institut, Politeknik, Akademi): - Direct JSON-RPC/REST connection to campus Neo Feeder servers (GetToken, InsertMahasiswa, InsertKRSMahasiswa, InsertNilai, InsertAKM). - Semester Academic Activity reporting (Aktivitas Kuliah Mahasiswa / AKM: IPS, IPK, SKS). 2.
